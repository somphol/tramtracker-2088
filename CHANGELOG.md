## [1.2.1] — 2026-11-01

### Added

- Auto driver that serves every platform, works the doors and yields to manual input.
- Separate brake and reverse controls with brake priority.
- 21-stop, 1,346 m Hoddle Grid loop with the Shrine, Southbank, QVM, Town Hall and
  tourist stops.
- Passenger stop requests, destination manifests, alighting animations and Myki scoring.
- Manual doors, door/traction interlock, en-AU stop/arrival/door announcements and
  subtitles.
- Driver's cab view with drag-look, speed FOV, curve lean and rain wipers.
- Stop guide cards with nearby places and Wikipedia links.
- Layered city soundscape and three weather presets.
- Minimizable mobile telemetry and stop-guide panels.
- Save schema, nine badges, JSON export/import and the 33-check self-test.
