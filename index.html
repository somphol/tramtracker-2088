How to run the 2088

Drive

W/↑ apply traction. S/↓ brake, then reverse. The tram is rail-guided — it steers itself. Slow for curves or the wheels will sing.

Serve stops

Each stop has a glowing platform marker. Roll in below 2 km/h inside the zone and the doors open automatically. Every passenger taps on with a Myki beep and adds to your score.

Bell

B rings the iconic double-ding. It is legally required to be delightful.

Driver view

V switches between the chase camera and a first-person driver's cab. Drag the scene to look around; the view eases back to the road when you let go. In rain the wipers run.

Hook Turn Challenge

Press G or the toolbar button. Drive the car into the painted hook turn box on the far-left corner, wait for the tram to clear, then complete the right turn across the tracks. Collisions end the attempt.

Weather

Switch between Classic Melbourne Sun, Four Seasons in One Day (sudden rain and lightning) and Twilight Yarra River Glow at any time.

Progress

Everything saves to this browser. Use Save to download tramtracker_save.json, and Load to restore a career on another device.

Tip: C recentres the chase camera behind the tram.
